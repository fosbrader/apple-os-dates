The surviving evidence is genuinely conflicting and does not yet justify
silently changing the date:

- MacRumors' original developer article is dated 2016-11-14 and has an

  <https://www.macrumors.com/2016/11/14/apple-seeds-ios-10-2-beta-3-to-developers/>
- A separate MacRumors article published at 1:02 p.m. Pacific on 2016-11-14

  <https://www.macrumors.com/2016/11/14/whats-new-in-ios-10-2-beta-3/>
- ThinkApple's 2016-11-15 report says the developer seed arrived “yesterday”
  and the public seed followed shortly afterward. That wording does not by

  <https://thinkapple.pl/2016/11/15/publiczna-beta-3-ios-10-2/>
- Neowin explicitly reports at 5:42 p.m. Eastern on 2016-11-15 that public
  testers received the new build “today” and developers received it

  <https://www.neowin.net/news/apple-releases-ios-102-and-macos-10122-public-beta-3-watchos-311-developer-beta-3/>
- iDevice.ro explicitly reports availability “today” on 2016-11-15:

  <https://www.redmondpie.com/ios-10.2-beta-3-all-new-changes-and-features-in-one-place-screenshots/>
- Geeky Gadgets reports at 8:13 a.m. on 2016-11-15 that it was available to

  <https://www.geeky-gadgets.com/apple-releases-ios-10-2-beta-3-15-11-2016/>
