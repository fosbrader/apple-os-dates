## Findings

- iOS 9.0 has three corroborated public appearances: July 9, July 22, and August 6, 2015.
- iOS 10.0 has seven corroborated public appearances: July 7, July 20, August 2, August 9, August 15, August 19, and August 26, 2016.
- Every paired developer route already exists in production.
- None of the ten exact public-beta identities exists in the published production snapshot queried at 2026-07-31T03:16:08.878Z.
- Builds and release-note change claims were deliberately not promoted in this chronology-only packet.

## Modeling notes

- “Public Beta 1” for iOS 9.0 is a normalized site label. Contemporary launch coverage establishes it as the initial public appearance, and subsequent coverage explicitly calls the July 22 appearance Public Beta 2.
- The iOS 10.0 Public Beta 4 source set retains the URL-slug and publication-time discrepancy instead of hiding it. Three publisher lineages support the August 9 appearance and fourth-public-beta identity when read together.
- Public appearances remain distinct events from developer appearances even when publishers report equivalent payloads.
